Security & Privacy

Trust starts with transparency.

We explain with rigor and clarity how document data is handled, free of hype or misleading claims.

dns

1. Where are my documents stored?

All data is stored exclusively in secure European Union infrastructure, in strict compliance with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards.

lock_person

2. Who can access my files?

Only you and users you explicitly authorize can access your files. The Payperless team never reads or inspects your private documents.

psychology

3. How does AI process content?

The extraction model runs in isolated sandboxes reading only required fields (e.g., vendor, date, amount). Your documents are never used to train public LLM models.

delete_forever

4. Can I export or delete my data?

Yes, at any time. Download all original documents and structured CSVs in one click, or permanently delete your account with zero hidden backups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Payperless works.

Payperless is a web application designed to centralize, organize, and manage your important documents — such as utility invoices, equipment warranties, and contracts. It uses vision AI to extract key data so you never lose proof of purchase or miss a renewal date.
Yes, no AI or OCR system is 100% infallible across every unusual receipt format. That is why Payperless is designed with human-in-the-loop oversight: we always display the original document side-by-side with extracted values, allowing you to edit or correct any field in one click before saving.
You can upload standard PDF documents as well as smartphone photos in JPG, PNG, and HEIC formats. The system automatically handles rotation and readability optimization for smooth parsing.
We do not believe in vendor lock-in. You can download a full archive containing all your original files in a single ZIP file alongside a structured CSV spreadsheet of all metadata whenever you choose, or request complete account erasure.
